FAQ

Common Questions

What budget do I need to start?
We recommend a minimum ad spend of ₹15,000–₹30,000/month to generate meaningful data. Our management fee is separate.
How quickly will I see results?
Paid ads can generate leads within days of launch. SEO and email marketing typically show strong results within 60–90 days.
Do you create the ad creatives too?
Yes — copywriting and creative design for ads is included in our management packages.
Which platforms do you manage?
We primarily manage Google, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), YouTube, and LinkedIn. We can also work with Snapchat and Twitter/X.
Can I pause or cancel anytime?
Yes. We work on monthly rolling contracts with 30-day notice. No lock-in contracts.
